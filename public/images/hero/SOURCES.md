## White shirt generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: SS Mensware website product card photograph, landscape 4:3. Input image: the user's attached photograph is the shirt reference. Create a polished photorealistic fashion ecommerce photograph of this exact white long-sleeved button-down shirt, retaining the white woven cotton texture, pointed collar, white buttons, and the small red heart above a red S embroidered on the left chest. Present only the shirt, neatly arranged front-facing on a simple wooden hanger against a warm light taupe studio backdrop. Full shirt and both sleeves visible, sleeves casually rolled as in the reference, balanced elegant natural folds and soft directional studio light, subtle realistic shadow. Center the garment with breathing room on all edges and keep the collar and embroidered detail in the upper middle where they remain visible in small cards; reserve a little quiet space at bottom for website text overlay. Remove the person, motorcycle, field and all outdoor surroundings. No added text, no new logos, no props, no watermark. Premium understated menswear catalogue photography, crisp cloth detail, white fabric clearly separated from background.
